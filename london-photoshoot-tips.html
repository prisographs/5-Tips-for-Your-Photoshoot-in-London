5 Tips for Your Photoshoot in London 倫敦拍照攻略五個貼士

Tip 01

Timing Is Everything — Go Early 選對時間：早上 + 避開人潮

London's light is most flattering in the morning and during golden hour — the first hour after sunrise or the last hour before sunset.
倫敦光線於早上及黃金時段（日出後一小時／日落前一小時）最為柔和。

  • Arrive at Tower Bridge or Westminster before 10am to beat the crowds. Tower Bridge 及 Westminster 建議於早上十時前抵達，以避開遊客人潮。
  • In winter, golden hour starts as early as 3:30pm. 冬季日落約四時，黃金時段可早至下午三時半開始。
  • In summer, you can shoot until 9pm — a huge advantage. 夏季則可拍攝至晚上九時，時間安排更為靈活。
Tip 02

Go Beyond Big Ben 善用倫敦獨特背景，不只限於 Big Ben

  • Notting Hill — Pastel-coloured townhouses and quiet lanes 彩色排屋與幽靜小巷，極具歐陸質感
  • Shoreditch — Street murals and raw industrial backdrops 街頭壁畫配合工業風背景，風格鮮明
  • Neal's Yard — A hidden colourful courtyard in Covent Garden 隱於 Covent Garden 的彩色庭院，遊客較少
  • South Kensington — Red-brick architecture with a classic London feel 紅磚建築散發濃厚的經典英倫氣息

Tip 04

Scout Ahead & Always Have a Plan B 提前勘察場地，並備妥後備方案

  • Use Google Street View to check the direction of light before you go. 善用 Google Street View 提前了解場地的光線方向。
  • Prepare 2–3 backup locations within walking distance of each other. 預備至少兩至三個步行距離內的後備拍攝地點。
  • Don't cancel for rain — wet pavements reflect light beautifully. 雨天亦毋須放棄——雨後地面的光線反射效果往往出人意料地美。
Tip 05

Know the Rules Before You Shoot 出發前了解拍攝規定

  • Private properties (e.g. inside Battersea Power Station) require a permit. 私人物業（如 Battersea Power Station 內部）須事先申請拍攝許可。
  • Royal Parks (Hyde Park, Kensington Gardens, etc.) require a permit for professional shoots. 皇家公園如進行專業拍攝，亦須申請許可。
  • Public streets are generally free, but tripods may require a permit in some areas. 公共街道一般無需許可，惟部分地點使用三腳架仍有限制。
  • The riverside beneath Tower Bridge (Southwark side) is private land — photographers with large cameras are regularly asked to leave. Tower Bridge 橋下 Southwark 一側屬私人土地，攜帶大型攝影器材者或會被要求離開。
This primarily applies to professional camera equipment. Always check the official website of each location before your visit for the latest regulations.

主要針對大型攝影器材，建議出發前先查閱該地點官方網站以了解最新規定。
